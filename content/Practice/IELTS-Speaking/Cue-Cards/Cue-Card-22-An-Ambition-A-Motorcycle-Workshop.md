# Cue Card 22 — An Ambition: A Motorcycle Workshop

**Study note:** Added model answer for practice towards IELTS Speaking Bands 7–9, not an examiner-scored response. First-person stories and altered quotations are anonymised study examples. Grammar notes retain qualifications and transcription uncertainty.

**Additional practice topic:** another way to develop your existing experiences and interests.

## Practice Cue Card

Describe an ambition you would like to achieve. Say what it is, how it developed, what you would need to do, and why it appeals to you.

## Extended Model Answer

One ambition I have is to become a motorcycle mechanic and eventually open my own workshop. It is an idea rather than a settled arrangement, so I wouldn't describe it as something I am about to do next month. What appeals to me is the combination of technical problem-solving, practical work and the possibility of running something of my own. I already have an interest in motorcycles, and I like imagining a role in which understanding a machine would lead to a visible, useful result for another person.

My career interests have changed over time. When I was a child, I wanted to become a scientist, and in high school I wanted to be an engineer. I now do technical work, so part of that interest in understanding how things work has continued. **In that sense**, a workshop wouldn't be a completely unrelated dream. The setting and skills would be different, but there would still be a problem to understand, possible explanations to consider and a need to check whether the solution actually worked rather than simply sounded convincing.

**Before I could make the idea realistic**, I would need proper mechanical training and practical experience. Being interested in riding a motorcycle is not the same as being qualified to repair one. I would want to learn from experienced people and understand the responsibilities involved before taking on customers' machines. **For example**, a repair is not only about identifying a fault; it also involves doing the work correctly and explaining to the owner what has been done. I would want the technical work and the communication to be equally dependable.

**Beyond the mechanical side**, running a workshop would involve money, customers and everyday organisation. I would need to understand local requirements, likely costs and whether there was enough demand for the service. The idea of being self-employed appeals to me because of the possibility of making more decisions about my own work. **That said**, I know that greater control doesn't automatically mean shorter hours or less stress. A business owner may have to deal with problems that an employee would normally pass to somebody else, especially at the beginning.

**For now**, I see the ambition as something worth exploring step by step. I don't need to turn an interesting possibility into an immediate promise. I could first learn more about the work and decide whether I enjoy the less glamorous parts as well as the appealing ones. **Ultimately**, the attraction is the chance to combine a personal interest with useful practical expertise. If I did pursue it, I would want to build something reliable and sustainable, rather than assume that opening a shop would automatically create the work–life balance I would like.

## Linking Language

**In that sense** connects ideas; **before** establishes a prerequisite; **for example** develops; **beyond** adds another aspect; **that said** qualifies; **for now / ultimately** distinguish present thinking and the broader goal.

## Your Grammar Review

| Reported wording | Correction | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “When I was a child... I want to be a scientist.” | When I was a child, I **wanted** to be a scientist. | Past time frame. |
| “I want the driving skill is about my motorcycle driving skill” | I **want to improve my motorcycle riding skills**. | **Want + to-infinitive** and a clear object. |

**Vocabulary:** **Ride a motorcycle**, **repair a motorcycle**, and **run a workshop** describe different activities. **I'm opening a workshop** would imply an arrangement not established in your notes.

## Shorten for the Exam

Keep the ambition, how it connects with your interests, two necessary steps and your main reason. Shorten the discussion of business risks and avoid presenting a hypothetical plan as a confirmed arrangement.
