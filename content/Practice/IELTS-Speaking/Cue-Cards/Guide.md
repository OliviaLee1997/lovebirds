# Cue Card Practice Guide

**Privacy note:** Personal and third-party details have been removed or generalised. First-person stories and altered quotations are anonymised study examples, not a biographical record or verbatim transcript.

**22 topics · 3–5-minute practice answers · highlighted linking language · personal grammar corrections**

These are **added study models for practice towards IELTS Speaking Bands 7–9**, not examiner-scored scripts. The practice questions and expanded descriptions are newly written; personal reflections and some illustrative details should be adjusted to match your experience. The error tables contain expressions recorded in your notes, which may include transcription uncertainty. Suggested improvements are distinguished from clear grammar errors.

Each extended answer is approximately **420–470 words**. At roughly 100–140 words per minute, this supports about **3–5 minutes of practice**; time your own delivery. Official IELTS Part 2 gives you one minute to prepare and **one to two minutes to speak**. Each topic includes a way to shorten the extended answer for that setting.

**Bold wording inside the model answers** highlights linking words, time markers and useful phrases that organise ideas. Some, such as **What I appreciate most is…**, are focus structures rather than conjunctions. Use them where the meaning fits; you do not need to memorise every highlighted phrase.

Higher-band speaking depends on **fluency and coherence, lexical resource, grammatical range and accuracy, and pronunciation**, equally weighted. For a Band 7 goal, prioritise sustained answers and frequent accurate sentences. Towards Band 8, develop ideas more precisely and use a wider range of structures with most sentences accurate. Band 9 also requires consistently flexible language and effortless, fully intelligible delivery. A longer answer or a particular connector does not establish a score.
