# Cue Card 12 — A Favourite Photograph: School Friends

**Study note:** Added model answer for practice towards IELTS Speaking Bands 7–9, not an examiner-scored response. First-person stories and altered quotations are anonymised study examples. Grammar notes retain qualifications and transcription uncertainty.

## Practice Cue Card

Describe a photograph you like. Say what it shows, when and where it was taken, who took it, and why it is important to you.

## Extended Model Answer

One of my favourite photographs is a picture of my high-school friends and me at our school gate. There are four of us in the photograph, and it was taken by the person working at the entrance. I have kept it on my phone for several years. It isn't important to me because it is a technically exceptional photograph. What matters is the connection between the people in the picture, the stage of life it represents and the friendship that has continued since then.

The school gate is a meaningful setting because it immediately places the photograph in a familiar part of our lives. When I look at it, I don't just see four people standing together; I think of the time when we belonged to the same school and saw one another as part of our ordinary routine. **Back then**, staying in touch didn't require much organisation. **By contrast**, once people leave school and develop separate lives, seeing each other often takes a deliberate decision and quite a bit of planning.

We have remained friends, and we try to meet occasionally. **Recently**, our commitments have changed, so arranging a time when everyone is free has become harder. That doesn't necessarily mean the friendship matters less. It means there are more responsibilities and schedules to consider. **In fact**, the effort involved can make a meeting feel more valuable. You are no longer together simply because the school timetable has put you in the same place; you are choosing to make room for one another.

**What I like about the photograph is** that it connects those two periods. It records a particular moment, but the relationships haven't remained frozen in that moment. We have grown older and our circumstances have changed, while the memory of being close at school is still something we share. I find that combination comforting. **Even if** we cannot meet as easily as before, the photograph reminds me that our connection has a history. It is a small, accessible reminder of something that would be difficult to capture in a single message.

**Looking back**, I am glad I kept it instead of treating it as just another image among many. Phones make it easy to accumulate photographs, but only some continue to mean something years later. This one does because it brings together a place, a group of people and a feeling of familiarity. If I had to explain its value in one sentence, I would say that it reminds me how friendship can continue while everyday life changes. **That's why** it remains one of the photographs I would be most reluctant to lose.

## Linking Language

**Back then / recently** locate time; **by contrast** compares life stages; **in fact** develops or adjusts a point; **even if** concedes; **looking back / that's why** reflect and explain.

## Your Grammar Review

| Reported wording | Correction | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “The picture… stored there for several years.” | **I've kept the picture on my phone for several years.** | Continuing possession: **have + past participle**. The recorded fragment may omit words. |
| “We are best friends in high school.” | We **were** best friends in high school. / We **have been** close friends **since** high school. | Choose a past state or a continuing relationship. |

**Added practice contrast, not another recorded mistake:** **for ten years** gives a duration; **since high school** gives a starting point. **The photo was taken…** uses the past passive.

## Shorten for the Exam

Keep what the photo shows, the school gate and photographer, the approximate period, and the continuing friendship. Cut the wider discussion of adult schedules and digital photos. Update the duration to match the date when you practise.
